# Unison

> Unison provides innovative residential equity sharing agreements and home financing solutions, allowing homeowners to access cash without the burden of monthly payments or interest.

## Core Products & Services
- [Equity Sharing Agreement](https://www.unison.com/equity-sharing-agreement): Get up to $500k in cash with no monthly payments and no interest in exchange for a share of your home's future change in value.
- [Equity Sharing Home Loan](https://www.unison.com/equity-sharing-home-loan): A home equity loan with low, interest-only monthly payments and a fixed, below-market rate.
- [How it Works](https://www.unison.com/how-it-works): A step-by-step guide to the equity sharing process, from appraisal to the 30-year partnership term.
- [Access Equity](https://www.unison.com/access-equity): Specific use cases for equity including debt consolidation, retirement funding, and home improvements.
